Privacy Policy
Last updated: March 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you register and use XSSniper, we collect:

  • Account data: Username, email address, hashed password
  • Scan data: Target URLs you submit, scan results, and log output
  • Usage preferences: Scan configuration settings saved to your profile
  • OAuth data: If you sign in with Google or GitHub, we receive your public profile (username, email, avatar)

2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

  • Provide and operate the XSSniper scanning service
  • Send account-related emails (verification, password reset)
  • Display your scan history and statistics on your dashboard
  • Save your preferences across sessions and devices

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored in a Supabase (PostgreSQL) database with row-level security enabled. Passwords are never stored in plain text — they are hashed using PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 with 600,000 iterations and a random salt.

4. Scan Data

Target URLs and scan logs you submit are stored to provide your scan history. You may delete individual scans or clear all history at any time from the Settings page.

5. Cookies and Sessions

We use HTTP-only session cookies to maintain your login state. We also use localStorage to cache preferences locally. We do not use tracking cookies or third-party analytics.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: View all your stored data through the dashboard
  • Rectification: Update your username and email in Settings
  • Erasure: Delete your account and all associated data from Settings → Danger Zone
  • Portability: Export your scan data as CSV from the History page

7. Data Retention

Your data is retained for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, all personal data including scan history is permanently removed from our systems.

8. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including HTTPS enforcement, HTTP security headers (CSP, HSTS, X-Frame-Options), session fixation prevention, and SSRF protection.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify you of significant changes by posting a notice on the platform.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to exercise your rights, please contact us through the platform's support channels.
